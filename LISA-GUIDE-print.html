Updating the Shop: Lisa’s Guide

Add, change and remove products yourself, no code needed. Saves show up on the website in about 1–2 minutes, and every save is kept in a history, so anything can be undone.

1Log in

  1. Go to knottygirlzboutique.com/admin (bookmark it!).
  2. Click Sign In with GitHub.
  3. If GitHub asks, type your GitHub username and password. The very first time, also click the green Authorize button.
  4. Click Shop on the left, then Shop Products.
Sign-in stuck?
  • GitHub may have opened a small sign-in window hiding behind your browser. Look on your taskbar or dock, finish signing in there, and it closes by itself.
  • Firefox says “Invalid state key”? Click the shield icon left of the address bar, turn Enhanced Tracking Protection off for this site, and try again. Or just use Google Chrome.

3Add a product

  1. Click + Add Product at the top of the list. A new, empty product opens at the top.
  2. Fill it in:
    • Product name, e.g. Pink Starfish Tote
    • Price, exactly how customers should see it, e.g. $20 or $5 each
    • Category: pick one from the list
    • Photo: click Browse and pick a picture. Square looks best. Big phone photos are fine (they’re shrunk automatically).
    • Photo description: a few words, e.g. Pink canvas tote with a white starfish
    • Description (optional): leave empty unless you want a line under the name
  3. Click the blue Save button (top right).

4Change a price

(or the name, photo or category)

  1. Find the product. Tip: press Ctrl+F (⌘+F on a Mac) and type part of the name.
  2. Click the › at the left end of its bar to open it.
  3. Change the Price box.
  4. Click the blue Save button.

5Remove a product

  1. Find the product.
  2. Click the ✕ at the right end of its grey bar.
  3. A blue “Item removed.” message appears. Wrong one? Click Restore.
  4. Click the blue Save button.
Just sold out for now? Remove it and add it again later. The photo stays saved.
Nothing changes on the website until you click Save. After saving, wait 1–2 minutes and refresh the Shop page.
Stuck or made a mistake? Don’t worry. Just text or call Jason. Everything can be undone.
