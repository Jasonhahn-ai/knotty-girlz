# Updating the Knotty Girlz Shop: Lisa's Guide

You can add, change and remove products yourself. No code needed. Anything you save shows up on the website
**in about 1–2 minutes**. Nothing can be permanently broken: every save is kept in a history, so Jason can always undo it.

---

## Log in

1. Go to **knottygirlzboutique.com/admin** (bookmark it!).
2. Click **Sign In with GitHub**.
3. If GitHub asks, type your GitHub username and password. The very first time, it also asks you to **Authorize**. Click the green button.
4. Click **Shop** on the left, then **Shop Products**.

> **Sign-in stuck?**
> - GitHub may have opened a small sign-in window that's hiding behind your browser. Look for it on your taskbar or dock, finish signing in there, and it will close by itself.
> - Using Firefox and seeing **"Invalid state key"**? Click the **shield icon** at the left of the address bar, turn **Enhanced Tracking Protection** off for this site, then try again. Or just use Google Chrome.

You'll see your products as a list of grey bars. Each bar has a number, and the product's photo, name and price show just below it.

```
 ›  12                          ═            ⋮   ✕
    [photo]  Cat Mom Tee ($15)
```

- **›** (left end): opens the product so you can change it
- **═** (middle): drag to move the product up or down. On a phone or tablet you'll see **↑ ↓** arrows instead.
- **⋮**: extra options, like **Duplicate** (makes a copy, handy for a similar product)
- **✕** (right end): removes the product

> Works on a computer, tablet or phone.

---

## Add a product

1. Click **+ Add Product** at the top of the list. A new, empty product opens at the top.
2. Fill it in:
   - **Product name**, e.g. *Pink Starfish Tote*
   - **Price**: type it exactly how customers should see it, e.g. *$20* or *$5 each*
   - **Category**: pick one from the list
   - **Photo**: click **Browse** and choose a picture from your computer or phone. Square photos look best.
     (Big phone photos are fine. They get shrunk automatically.)
   - **Photo description**: a few words about the picture, e.g. *Pink canvas tote with a white starfish*
   - **Description (optional)**: leave it empty unless you want a line of text under the name
3. Click the blue **Save** button (top right).

---

## Change a price (or name, photo, category)

1. Find the product in the list.
   Tip: press **Ctrl+F** (**⌘+F** on a Mac) and type part of the name to jump to it.
2. Click the **›** at the left end of its grey bar to open it.
3. Change the **Price** box.
4. Click the blue **Save** button (top right).

---

## Remove a product

1. Find the product in the list.
2. Click the **✕** at the **right end of its grey bar**.
3. A blue **"Item removed."** message appears. Clicked the wrong one? Click **Restore** in that message.
4. Click the blue **Save** button (top right).

> Just sold out for now? You can remove it and add it again later. The photo stays saved.

---

## Good to know

- **Nothing changes on the website until you click Save.**
- After saving, wait 1–2 minutes, then refresh the Shop page to see it.
- Products show on the Shop page in the same order as this list.
- **Stuck or made a mistake?** Don't worry. Just text or call Jason. Everything can be undone.
