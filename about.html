Our Story

A little sand, a little salt, and a whole lot of love.

🌸 Hi, I’m Lisa! 🌸

Welcome to my boutique! I’m so happy you’re here! 💕

I started my small business in beautiful Cape Charles, VA, in 2021 with a passion for fashion, creativity, and helping others find something special.

I offer a little bit of everything, including stylish shirts, pants, tunics, beautiful jewelry, seasonal holiday items, and custom-designed shirts!

Whether you’re shopping for yourself, searching for the perfect gift, or looking for something unique, I hope you’ll find something you love!

Thank you for supporting my small business and allowing me to do what I love. Every purchase, share, and kind word means the world to me! ❤️

🛍️ Happy shopping, and I look forward to helping you find your next favorite thing!

💕 Lisa
